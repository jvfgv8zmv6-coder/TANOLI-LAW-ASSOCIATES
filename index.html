About Our Firm

Committed to careful preparation, clear advice, and client-focused legal representation.

Tanoli Law Associates assists clients in understanding their legal options and navigating legal proceedings. Every matter is approached with professionalism, confidentiality, and attention to the client's circumstances.

Practice Areas

Legal advice and representation tailored to the needs of each matter.

Service Law

Advice and representation in employment and public-service disputes, including disciplinary proceedings, service benefits, promotions, and related appeals.

Criminal Law

Representation in criminal proceedings, including bail applications, defence strategy, and related court matters.

Civil Law

Assistance with civil disputes, property and contractual matters, legal claims, and related litigation.

Family Law

Guidance and representation in family matters, including divorce, child custody, maintenance, and related disputes.

Tax Law

Support with tax-related advice, notices, compliance issues, disputes, and representation before relevant forums.

FIA Matters

Legal assistance in matters involving the Federal Investigation Agency, including inquiries, investigations, and proceedings.

Terrorism Cases

Representation in cases under applicable anti-terrorism laws, including bail and defence proceedings where appropriate.
